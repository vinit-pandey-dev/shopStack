# ShopStack store policies

This file is the knowledge base for the ShopStack AI assistant. Each "## Heading" section below becomes one
searchable chunk, so keep every section self-contained and start it with the answer. Text above the first
"##" heading is ignored. The figures (days, fees, hours, email) are sample values: edit them to match your
real store, then restart the backend so the assistant re-reads this file.

## Shipping and delivery
ShopStack offers free standard shipping on every order, with no minimum order value. Orders are processed and dispatched within 2 business days after the order is confirmed. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Estimated delivery time is 3 to 7 business days after dispatch, depending on your location. Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed. We currently ship within India only. Orders are shipped to the address entered at checkout, so please check it before placing the order, because the address cannot be changed after the order is placed.

## Order status and tracking
Every order moves through these stages: Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered. Orders can also be Cancelled. You can follow your order on the Orders page, where a tracker shows the current stage. Each status change also sends you a notification under the bell icon. Confirmed means we received your order and payment. Shipped means the order has left our warehouse. Delivered means the order reached your address.

## Payment methods
ShopStack accepts credit and debit cards, UPI, and cash on delivery. Card and UPI payments are charged at checkout and the order is confirmed immediately. With cash on delivery you place the order without paying online and pay in cash when the order arrives. Your payment status then shows as pay on delivery until the order is delivered. We never store full card details. Prices are shown in US dollars. ShopStack is a demonstration store: payments are simulated and no real money is charged.

## Failed or declined payments
If a payment is declined at checkout, no order is created and you are not charged. Your items stay in your cart, so nothing is lost. Common reasons are an incorrect card number, insufficient funds, or the bank declining the payment. You can try again with another card or UPI ID, or choose cash on delivery instead. If the problem continues, contact support.

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order yourself from the Orders page by clicking Cancel order, as long as the order has not shipped yet. Once an order is Shipped or Delivered it can no longer be cancelled online. In that case, contact support with your order number. When an order is cancelled, the items go back into stock and you receive a notification confirming the cancellation. Cancelling is free of charge.

## Refunds
If you cancel an order that you already paid for by card or UPI, the full amount is refunded to the original payment method, and the order shows a Refunded payment status. Refunds are normally completed within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank. Cash on delivery orders that are cancelled before delivery have nothing to refund because no payment was taken. Shipping is free, so there are no shipping charges to refund.

## Returns and exchanges
You can request a return within 7 days of delivery if the item is unused and in its original packaging. To start a return, contact support with your order number and the item you want to return. Returns are checked before they are approved. Approved returns are refunded to the original payment method. Items that are damaged by the customer, used, or missing their original packaging may be refused. Some items, such as personal care products that have been opened, cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
If your order arrives damaged, defective, or not what you ordered, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo of the problem. We will replace the item or give a full refund, and we cover the return shipping in these cases.

## Account and security
You need a free account to add items to your cart and place orders. Create one with a username, email and password. Passwords are stored in encrypted form and cannot be read by anyone, including us. Keep your password private, and log out when using a shared computer. Your order and payment updates appear under the bell icon in the top bar.

## Privacy
We collect only the information needed to run your account and deliver your orders, such as your name, email, shipping address and order history. We do not sell your personal information. We do not store full card details.

## Contact and support
For help with an order, payment, return or refund, contact support with your order number. Support is available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM India Standard Time, and we aim to reply within one business day. Sample support email: support@shopstack.example.
