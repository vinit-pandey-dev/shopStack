# ShopStack store information

This file is the "knowledge base" for the AI assistant. Edit the text below to match your store,
then restart the backend. Each "## Heading" section becomes one searchable chunk.

## Shipping and delivery
Shipping is free on every order with no minimum. Orders are dispatched within 2 business days of being confirmed.
Once an order is marked as shipped you receive a notification, and the order tracker on the Orders page moves to Shipped.

## Payment methods
ShopStack accepts credit or debit cards, UPI, and cash on delivery. Card and UPI payments are charged at checkout and the order is confirmed immediately.
With cash on delivery the order is also confirmed immediately, but you pay in cash when the order arrives. Card details are never stored by the shop.
This is a demo store, so no real money is charged.

## Failed or declined payments
If a payment is declined at checkout, no order is created, you are not charged, and the items stay in your cart. You can retry with another card, UPI ID or choose cash on delivery.

## Order status and tracking
Every order moves through Confirmed, Shipped and Delivered. You can follow this on the Orders page, and each change sends you a notification under the bell icon.
An order can also be Cancelled, in which case the stock is returned and any card or UPI payment is refunded.

## Cancellations and refunds
To cancel an order, contact support with your order number. When an order is cancelled the payment is refunded to the original payment method and you receive a notification.
Orders that have already been delivered cannot be cancelled.

## Account and notifications
Create a free account with a username, email and password to add items to your cart and place orders. Your order and payment updates appear under the bell icon.

## Contact and support
For help with an order, contact support and include your order number. Support replies within one business day.
